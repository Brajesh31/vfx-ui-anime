# 🌱 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We aim to make participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and attribution.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may block participants who repeatedly violate this policy. Report concerns privately through the repository's available contact channels.
